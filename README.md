## About

I am a software engineer and M.Sc. student in Artificial Intelligence at Islamic Azad University, with 4+ years of experience in backend engineering, distributed systems, DevOps, and applied machine learning. I lead backend and AI systems development for a U.S. medical toxicology practice, where I own **ToxLumen**, an AI-enabled clinical toxicology platform.

My research examines the **reliability and robustness of AI systems in clinical decision-making**, with a particular focus on tool-using large language models.

## Current Research

**M.Sc. Thesis (in progress)** — *Decision-Boundary Robustness in Tool-Using Clinical LLMs: A Counterfactual Benchmark in Clinical Toxicology*<br/>
Islamic Azad University · Supervisor: Dr. Yahya Forghani · Advisor: Dr. Omid Mehrpour (Medical Toxicologist)

- **Question:** Do tool-using clinical LLM agents recognize when a small but clinically decisive variable crosses a meaningful decision boundary, and change their decisions accordingly?
- **Method:** A structured counterfactual benchmark of minimal case pairs in clinical toxicology — each pair holds the clinical context fixed and alters a single decision-relevant variable.
- **Evaluation:** Boundary recognition, unsafe use of invalid tool outputs, triage behavior, and next-step routing — together with runtime guards and structured decision reporting, to determine whether agents fail safely, fail silently, or correctly redirect the clinical workflow.

### Research Interests

- **Clinical AI & Decision Support** — safety, validity, and clinically meaningful behavior of AI systems that support or evaluate clinical decisions.
- **Reliable & Robust Large Language Models** — decision-boundary behavior, counterfactual sensitivity, and the safe use of tool-augmented models.
- **AI-Enabled Software Systems** — engineering production software that integrates ML and generative AI into real-world workflows.
- **Distributed Systems, MLOps & Cloud Infrastructure** — reproducibility, asynchronous processing, containerization, and production ML systems.
